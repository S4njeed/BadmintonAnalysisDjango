Recovery

Tips for Best Results

  • Record from the side

    A clean side angle makes the body line and racket path easier to read.

  • Use strong lighting

    Good contrast gives the estimator more reliable detail across fast motion.

  • Keep the player centered

    Centering the action reduces edge cropping and keeps the body in frame.

  • Try slow motion when possible

    Slower footage helps highlight the contact point and follow-through path.

  • Increase frame skip for long clips

    Higher skip values keep processing responsive when the source footage is lengthy.
