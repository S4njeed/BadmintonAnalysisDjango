🏸 Badminton Shot Tutorial

Here's a quick guide to the basic badminton shots:

The smash is the most powerful offensive shot, hit downwards towards the opponent's court.

Key Steps:
  1. Preparation: Get behind the shuttlecock, turn your body sideways, non-racket arm pointing up.
  2. Swing: Raise your racket arm high, elbow bent. Swing forward and upward, contacting the shuttlecock at the highest point possible.
  3. Contact: Hit the shuttlecock forcefully downwards, snapping your wrist at the point of impact.
  4. Follow-through: Continue the swing downwards across your body.

Goal: End the rally quickly with an unreturnable shot.

The drop shot is a soft, deceptive shot that lands just over the net in the opponent's frontcourt.

Key Steps:
  1. Preparation: Similar setup to a smash or clear to deceive the opponent.
  2. Swing: Use a slower arm speed compared to a smash. Focus on control rather than power.
  3. Contact: Gently tap or slice the shuttlecock, aiming for it to just clear the net and drop sharply.
  4. Follow-through: Minimal follow-through to maintain control.

Goal: Catch the opponent off guard, force them to move forward, or set up another shot.

The clear is a defensive shot hit high and deep into the opponent's backcourt.

Key Steps:
  1. Preparation: Get under the shuttlecock, turn sideways.
  2. Swing: Swing the racket upwards and forwards, similar to throwing a ball.
  3. Contact: Hit the shuttlecock high and in front of your body, aiming for a high trajectory.
  4. Follow-through: Follow through fully in the direction of the shot.

Goal: Create time to recover position, push the opponent back, or reset the rally.
