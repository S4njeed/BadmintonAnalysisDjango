Recovery

Tips for Best Results

  • Record from the side

    A side angle makes racket path, shoulder rotation, and body alignment easier to read.

  • Use strong lighting

    Good contrast helps the shuttle, limbs, and court lines stay visible through fast motion.

  • Keep the player centered

    Centering the athlete keeps the swing and recovery path inside the court frame.

  • Try slow motion when possible

    Slower footage helps highlight contact point, shuttle height, and follow-through.

  • Increase frame skip for long clips

    Higher skip values keep analysis responsive when a rally or session runs long.
